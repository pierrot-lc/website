Today I got diagnosed a pretty bad disease, something that could kill me in the long run.
Thankfully, it is evolving slowly, and probably won't kill me before getting to an age where I'll be
okay with it.

I don't know how to feel about it. Multiple thoughts at the same time. I want something to be
remembered for when I die. a scientific accomplishment? or maybe just a good video game? I want my
family and friends to have something to say about me, something they can be proud of.

I should read that book that my father made for his deceased friend that was an artist.

It turns out doing a PhD is not that important after all. You should enjoy the time you have, and
use it meaningfully. I should share more. Participate to open source projects. Write about something
non-trivial that I did or simply know about. Defend my ideas and what I believe to be important.

I want to die **proud**.

_I also want to cry._
