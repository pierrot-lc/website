Last time I thought that ALiBi was my only friend. Well turns out when you randomly sample a city to
add to your tour, it might not be the best thing to use. RoPE trains much *much* faster, actually!

two other nodes, or you're linked to yourself). My model is fast and accurate at TSP-100. Sadly,
this same model is worse at 10,000. I need somehow to fight a shift in how the model specializes at
his training scale. Similarly to my previous attempts, my model is having good scores at 10,000 at
the beginning, and after some time, it starts to diverge. Should I go RL? I'm trying but it gets
soooo much time to train.
