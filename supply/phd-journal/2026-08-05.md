This is now the third time that my PENS paper got rejected. I really thought that my reviewers would
raise their scores with my latest results, but they clearly already made up their mind. At least I
now have two new baselines and a new sparsification result where I have new interesting things to
say.

If I had to guess, I'd say that my paper is considered too simple and not novel enough. I naively
thought that being state-of-the-art (and explaining that it is for good reasons) was sufficient to
be published, but I can understand that NeurIPS only wants hardcore papers?
