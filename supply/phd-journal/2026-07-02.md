Turns out I am in a slightly better health condition than expected. That thing that _could_ kill me
in the long run _might_ never evolve past the current state of the affairs. I still have updated my
life perspectives. Tomorrow I'll be on holidays with some friends!

My insertion model is not going very well, it struggles to generalize correctly. I am not sure why,
I am less and less liking the idea. I believe attention is not enough, I need something that
naturally handle sequence-length generalization. For now, my best ally is an ALiBi-like bias in the
self-attention operation.

This work also made me think about the best way to encode a cycle. This is another interesting
side-quest if I want in some later work to embed a full-blown solution into some latent space. This
could have a lot of applications, for example for diffusion/flow and looped models.
